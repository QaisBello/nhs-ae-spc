How to read the chart
Grey points are common cause: the normal variation of a stable process. Reacting to them is chasing noise.
Blue is special cause improvement, orange special cause concern: a point outside the limits, a run of 7 above or below the average, 7 rising or falling, or 2 of 3 close to a limit.
A target above the upper limit will consistently be missed; inside the limits, hitting it is down to chance.

From 82 downloads to one table

NHS England publishes one file a month, and the format has changed over time. src/clean.py reconciles them and logs every fix. The check that matters: recomputed 2025-26 performance should match the National Audit Office's published figure.

Source: NHS England, A&E Attendances and Emergency Admissions, monthly provider files, Jan 2020 – Sep 2026. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Four-hour performance counts all A&E types, booked appointments included, as NHS England reports it.
Method: XmR charts following NHS England's Making Data Count guidance, with the rules of the NHS-R Community's NHSRplotthedots package (screened moving ranges, limits at 2.66 × the average moving range). "12-hour waits" are from decision to admit to admission, not from arrival. Targets: 95% constitutional standard; 82% interim target for 2026/27.
